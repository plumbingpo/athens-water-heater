Hot Water for Kitchens, Restaurants, and Food Facilities

Dish machines, sanitizing sinks, and handwashing stations all compete for the same hot water, and an undersized or failing heater reveals itself as a dish machine that can't hit temperature and stacks of dirty plates. A high-temperature dish machine usually needs final rinse water near 180°F, which almost always calls for a booster heater working alongside the main supply. We handle high-input tanks, tankless racks, booster heaters, and peak-demand sizing, and we schedule every visit around your prep and service times.

A dish room without enough hot water can't sanitize, and health inspectors notice lukewarm handwashing sinks right away. We keep temperatures and recovery where your operation needs them, then leave behind written reports and service logs after each visit. Those records show a well-maintained kitchen to inspectors and landlords.

Replacements and maintenance visits are planned with owners, managers, and kitchen staff, so everything fits the hours you're open. When a job can't happen during service, we install overnight or before opening, so hot water is ready when the first shift arrives.

Hotels & Multifamily

Large-Facility and Multi-Unit Hot Water Service

Scale changes everything. One failed tank in a hotel can mean cold showers for an entire floor, and an apartment building with a single central heater has no margin for error. Athens Water Heater provides the know-how and planning these systems require, from recirculation pumps and mixing valves to staged installations.

For a large facility, downtime is measured in refunds and tenant complaints, so our approach is built around prevention. That means comprehensive system assessments, sizing matched to real occupancy, multiple heaters piped in parallel so a single failure never cuts off hot water, recirculation service, and scheduled flushing and descaling to hold capacity. If a unit does go down, our water heater repair technicians isolate it so the rest of the system keeps running.

Safety

Installation, Inspection, and Record-Keeping

Badly installed or neglected hot water equipment can create carbon monoxide hazards, scalding, bacterial growth in lukewarm storage, and costly water damage from a ruptured tank. That's a real liability for a commercial property owner responsible for employees, customers, and tenants. Our commercial installations and inspections cover venting, combustion air, gas line sizing, relief valve discharge, expansion tanks, mixing valves, and code items like drain pans and seismic straps where required, and each visit ends with a written report listing risks, priorities, and recommended preventive steps.

Regular professional service protects your people and your property, and acting on expert recommendations keeps small issues from becoming liabilities. It also builds a paper trail. We keep service logs, temperature records, and a binder of reports for every account, and we increase visit frequency for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, laundries, and mechanical rooms. When a landlord, insurer, or inspector asks for proof of due diligence, it's ready.

Fast Commercial Water Heater Service

A hot water failure on a commercial property can shut down a kitchen, cancel a day of salon appointments, leave hotel guests with cold showers, and expose the owner to tenant disputes. Athens Water Heater provides fast emergency water heater service for commercial properties across Athens and surrounding areas, and we schedule non-urgent work after hours or before opening so your doors stay open.

Every Athens Water Heater program is customized to the property's equipment, usage, and budget, and covers scheduled inspections, flushing, anode rod checks, descaling for tankless units, relief valve tests, burner and vent inspection, recirculation and mixing valve checks, and verification of delivered water temperature.

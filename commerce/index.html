Getting More Years From Your Water Heater in Commerce

The best way to avoid a surprise leak is routine maintenance. We recommend that homeowners flush the tank once a year (more often with hard water), have the anode rod checked every three to five years, test the relief valve once a year to make sure it isn't stuck, and keep the thermostat around 120 degrees, which reduces the risk of scalding and cuts down on scale. Tankless owners should plan on descaling every year in hard-water areas.

Age matters too. Most tank water heaters run 8 to 12 years, tankless units often reach 20 with regular descaling, and heat pump models typically last 10 to 15. If your heater is past that window and showing symptoms such as rusty hot water, rumbling during heating, dampness under the tank, or hot water that runs out sooner than it used to, a planned replacement is almost always cheaper and far less stressful than an emergency one with water on the floor.

The sooner you call about a hot water problem, the smaller the repair tends to be. A tank that's just begun to rumble can often be fixed with a flush, yet the same sediment, ignored through a few more years, can overheat the bottom of the tank and burn out the lower element on an electric model, forcing a replacement much sooner. Save our number on your phone now so you can reach us fast as soon as something sounds or looks wrong. Early intervention saves time, money, and ruined floors.
